Attribution and use

Benjamin P Taylor’s synthesis and practice prompts draw on Peter Block, Barry Oshry, and the other authors named alongside their work. FONT is attributed through Doug Belshaw’s account of Outlandish and Abi Handley. The five core practices and four dynamics are connected accounts in my own teaching, with their wider influences explained in the source collections.

This guide is not an accredited Flawless Consulting course and does not replace the original books or licensed materials. Linked sources retain their own authorship and terms. You are welcome to share links to this guide and the downloadable practice notes with attribution. External links may move or require an account; inclusion is not an endorsement of every claim.
