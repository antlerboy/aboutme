The extended StretchCon material

This guide draws primarily on my long presentation, Cadenced culture: the key to high-performing teams based on trust, prepared for StretchCon at Compass Tech Summit, 6 October 2023. The main sequence develops the four dynamics, diagnosis, prescription, and rituals. The extended material includes systemic positions, power and love, VSM, essential balances, boundaries, and facilitation.

Sources and scope

The terms segment, blend, empower, and harmonise are my practical rendering of a pattern developed through Oshry’s work, read alongside Beer’s management cybernetics and group-process work. The connections page distinguishes the sources and links to their own accounts.

The diagnosis table, weight and rhythm maps, practices, and attention to rebounds come from the 2023 StretchCon material. The six relationship examples and the experiment form develop that material into a working aid. They’re propositions to test with the people involved. They don’t supply a validated score, a fixed ideal profile, or evidence that a named intervention will produce a particular outcome.

The source deck labels its case illustrations ‘confabulated, anonymised’. This guide uses illustrative examples on that basis and doesn’t treat them as documented client outcomes. Human-needs connections in the teaching material are ways of considering experience, rather than a claim that every person shares one exhaustively established list of needs.
