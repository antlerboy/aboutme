Feedback after the first Sunday

OXYGEN mail 2 · 20 February 1997 · Re: Dead air

Paul Harrod heard the whole show. He comments on the live performances, the introductions, and the repeated station ident.

Dead Air, heard it all, bloody good. You sounded relaxed and confident which is critical.

Maxie on the second programme

OXYGEN mail 4 · 24 February 1997 · Re: your mail

Maxie Haddow-Allen writes about editing the sessions, the sound of Katie Buckhaven’s performance, and an idea for a regular contribution.

Thought the show was better than the first - Katie came over better than Eoin had - mainly because she was better miked

Superfly’s acoustic session

OXYGEN mail 6 · 12 March 1997 · Re: Sunday

Chris Harding explains that the band, previously called Pants, can send two guitarists. He lists ‘Short Skirts’ and ‘Egg Beater Soul’ and leaves the third song open.

the two guitarists can do an acoustic session if you want

Superfly choose the third song

OXYGEN mail 8 · 13 March 1997 · Re: Sunday

Harding confirms the final selection and explains why the band will probably leave the bongos at home.

the third song will definitely be 'Circle-shaped'

Another half-hour

OXYGEN mail 12 · 15 March 1997 · Dead Air tomorrow

Maxie Haddow-Allen offers to extend the Sunday slot when the News Review drops out.

there will be no News Review tomorrow, so would you like to do another half an hour?

Before the first live Sunday

OXYGEN mail 23 · 6 February 1997 · Re: Hello stranger

Ben Taylor replies to Daniel Lester about getting involved with the new programme.

Dead Air is a complete rip-off of the Mark Radcliffe show (of which I am recording the last episode as we speak) - very good music, stupid 'comedy' from me, my co-presenter and producer, live acoustic music and live poetry. And stuff. It's just an hour and a half, but is on prime time on Sunday - 4-5.30pm. We will be doing four shows live from FEB 16, and recording 8 shows for next term over Easter.

Ben replies to Superfly

OXYGEN mail 37 · 12 March 1997 · Re: Sunday

Ben discusses the percussion, language on the show, and a possible Curtis Mayfield record.

Excellent about Superfly, I was immediately set against you with the name Pants! I'll probably try to play the Curtis Mayfield track!

Musicians and poets for the station

OXYGEN mail 47 · 26 April 1997 · Dead Air performers.

Ben wrote to Alex about performers contacted for Dead Air who might appear on Culture Zone. Ian Green’s entry describes his guitar and vocal session. The private directory also includes telephone numbers, availability, and personal comments.

Session going out on Dead Air this week, another session (3 songs) available on Minidisc.

Three Sundays in June

OXYGEN mail 48 · 27 May 1997 · Re: dead air

Ben’s message to Damian Smyth gives the intended order of the Dylan show, the farewell, and the Christmas special.

Er, okay, fine.
Sunday Week 7 (ie end of week six)
- Dylan : 2 musicians, one poet
entire show of various Dylan covers and originals- the big joke: we won't refer to that fact throughout.
Sunday Week 8
- Fairwell show : 'greatest hits', musician, poet
Various cheesy 'those were the days' features - and the big joke 'oh no, you mean it's *not* the last show?'
Sunday Week 9
- Christmas special : musician, poet
Christmas music and features and the 'Dead Air' Xmas Single - A-side a spoken-word cover of 'Eternal Flame' (in the style of William Shatner) by Mr Ben, B-side a Rufus Doors orchestral version of 'Every Loser Wins'. (Excellent choice for the playlist). Plus the Dead Air Xmas message to the nation. (Phil Spector style).

Ben + Rufus as the Eric+Ern for the 90s!
