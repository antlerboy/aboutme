Discover Oxford / Explore

DICOVER.DOC · Programme idea · Ben Taylor, St Anne’s · February 1995

Programme idea - Ben Taylor, St Anne's

Title: Discover Oxford / Explore

Summary: A travelogue-style programme, likely to be about a quarter of an hour in length, introducing listeners to a part of their own city in some depth.
Who lives there- what goes on there and a little as appropriate about history, architecture, local developments, whatever.
Pre-recorded.

Development: Many listeners to OSR know very little about their own city, this would attempt to give some genuine understanding of a certain section of it each time, eg Summertown, Barton, Jericho etc. There would be interviews and comments with people who live and work there, information about what goes on, what entertainment is available and what people think of it, and generally the same information about an area that would be sought for a travel show. A slightly comic approach ('the rough guide to Oxford') would be feasible but would be unlikely to lead to a desirable treatment of the subject-matter. One or two enthusiastic presenters would be needed, and the show would be pre- recorded largely on location and need quite a lot of editing. Educational and entertaining!

Nerds

INTERNET.DOC · Programme idea · Ben Taylor, St Anne’s · February 1995

Programme idea - Ben Taylor, St Anne's

Title: Nerds

* Tricky one this- really, really naff titles about dealing with the Internet and World-Wide Web are possible.

Summary: A weekly round-up of information available on the Internet and World-Wide Web, of which there is an enormous amount readily accessible, serious and entertaining, and other relevant features.

Development: Regular round-ups of certain interesting news-groups and pages, along with features on interesting areas available, spiced up with relevant clips and music.In addition there would be regular links with a few net-nerds from around the world, and an opportunity for interaction by listeners with their own E-mail accounts. * [This is a valuable option for all kinds of phone-ins, requests, or interaction of any kind since it is free and accessible to most students] There would be live and pre-recorded sections, twenty minutes or half an hour long, with a couple of presenters. The obvious technical requirement is at least two computers on the net, at least one of which would ideally have full multi-media capacity, which would allow down-loading of enormous amounts of usable music,fx etc.

Inspirations

INSPIRE.DOC · Programme idea · Ben Taylor, St Anne’s · February 1995

Programme idea - Ben Taylor, St Anne's

Title: Inspirations

Summary: A short, flexible programme suitable for either a regular slot or use as required. The slot would aim to give people something genuinely inspiring to think about, the most obvious parallel being Radio 4's "Thought for the Day", although we would use a much wider variety of sources and presenters.
Pre-recorded.

Development: The idea would be to provide a bit of inspiration through some quotation, poem, thought, recollection, story or aspiration of either practical or romantic value. As well as items selected by OSR, as wide a variety of people as possible would be asked to explain or summarise their own particular source of inspiration. Running time between 60 seconds and 5 minutes.
The emphasis would be on an inspiring thing, then an explanation of why it was special to someone.
A selection of examples might include:
- readings eg very short excerpts from Bible/Quran etc poetry- just a few lines (Kubla Khan...) song lyrics(an infinite choice of these) brief extracts from books or journalism
- excerpts from famous speeches or events, possibly with someone to briefly set the scene or explain what it means to them personally. eg ' I have a dream'
Lyndon Johnson on the Great Society
'One small step...'
'The crowd are on the pitch...'
- musical moments, and why they are special to people.
- advice or ideas, on quite a serious level (no 'Tips of the Century or whatever). eg Big Issue vendor- how I'd like to be treated and why religious or political thoughts
- 'my personal inspiration/motto'-type thing anyone from high-profile Union speakers to Ali of kebab-van fame.
The best way to collect these would be to ask all OSR people, when conducting interviews/out on the streets/making programmes, to try to get all guests to produce such snippets, then they could be collated centrally, added to specially commissioned readings/items, an with a few seconds' intro and outro be completed. Could be fun.

The Hidden Link

LINK.DOC · Programme idea · Ben Taylor, St Anne’s · February 1995

Programme idea - Ben Taylor, St Anne's

Title: The Hidden Link

Summary: A short, pre-recorded slot expounding old and new, original and unoriginal, existing and made-up conspiracy theories, and exposing hidden links (real or imaginary) between people and events in the news. Probably best with two regular presenters with fixed characters/roles.
