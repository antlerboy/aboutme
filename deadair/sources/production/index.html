The Ben Taylor Show

BTSHOW2.DOC · Proposal · file saved 29 November 1996

The Ben Taylor Show

Time: 1am-3am
Day: pos. Mon or Wed
Format: Music, DJ stuff, live music and poetry
Presenter: Ben Taylor
Producer: Maxie Allen
Show Title: ideas...
Highlands Oblivious (Remix) The Small Room / The Little Boy's Room Thick+Thin (he's thick... and I'm thin) Hugeing It Revolution Radio Radio Clash You know how we do
Theme tune: ideas...
Whole Lotta Love (London Symphony Orchestra Version)
King of the Road (Hammond Organ Goes Pop Country Version)
Pocorn (original or Phantom Surfers version)
Music:
charity shop records, punk/garage/new wave/psychedelic, extreme indie, country, roots reggae, very new music, anything... (DJ choice)
Features:
Live Poetry including talented local poets from 'The Catweazle Club', poetry societies, proper poets, poetic academics etc.
Live Acoustic Music band from the Union Bar acoustic session, Catweazle, local bands, proper bands
Music Anorakdom things about music of interest only to a very small minority
News from Pijnacker all the latest local gossip from this happening Dutch town
Musical links selections of songs (in sequence) with extremely vague connections
A particularly long song each week
An Aztec Camera song each week
Timechecks sample from the Beautiful South album that goes 'Our period of waiting is over. This is the time...' then recording of the speaking clock. From France. Wrong time.
Songs That Sound Better At The Wrong Speed
Etc

Plus a great selection of jingles sampled from various sources, comment on music and news if we feel like it, and a welcoming and friendly atmosphere - like being invited to an after-gig party with all your favourite bands, oh yes. Comic interplay between presenter and producer. Not at all like the Radcliffe Show, oh no. Much use of theme tunes. Amusing. Entertaining. Friendly. No playlist, no danger.
Ben Taylor, November 96

After the demo

AFTDEM.DOC · Debrief · file saved 13 December 1996

Dead Air

DEMO: 2 HOUR SHOW

We thought the demo was quite good, and these are ways we would make the show better:

Ben says he will remember to introduce what's on the show at the start, and polish up his links a bit. He wants to sound less of a prat, and for a start will never use the phrase "a word from our sponsors" again.

Maxie says he has to get his hand back in with getting the levels right. And we will make sure we talk into the microphones, and only at the right times.

Rufus will remember not to turn records off while they're still on air, unless it is deliberate and funny. Rufus will be involved more in the links, and steer away from the obscene and the illegal.

There will be a suitably respectful pause after performers have been on.

We will all stop laughing at things off-air.

We will have some more features (including phone things), which we couldn't do on the demo.

We might have a better title.

Rufus' story will be split into three sections.

Rufus will not mess around with the speed of records on air, even if they are very long and he does not like them.

We will have the time and resources to e.g. play the two versions of Dancing Queen and slip from one to the other coming in at the right point.

Jingles will be better; and will exist.

Still, not bad for the first time on air; things can only get better.

DeadAir DeadAir DeadAir DeadAir DEAD AIR!

Dead Air

An hour and a half of some blokes pissing about on the radio and playing records. Regular live sessions and live poetry, funny stuff and records. Wit, wisdom, philosophical aperçus and records.
