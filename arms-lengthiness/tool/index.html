Purpose and boundaries

The 21 requirements

Choose a whole number from 0 to 10. Every question has its own endpoints: a higher number doesn't always mean more independence. A slider starts in the middle without an answer; move it, or type a number, to choose.

For sponsor involvement in governance, 0 means full oversight, and 10 means no involvement. For community, service-user, and employee involvement, 0 means none, and 10 means full involvement.

Capability and feasibility

These judgements don't change the fit percentage. They identify work that the percentage cannot do.

Can we define outcomes, govern the relationships, and steer a complex system?
Can the necessary administrative and specialist support work in the proposed arrangement?
Is there evidence that the delivery body can govern itself and deliver? If no body is needed, explain that in the notes.
Is the wider market or community willing and able to sustain this arrangement?

Your comparison

These percentages compare your requirements with illustrative profiles. They don't test whether your requirements are justified, or whether any actual provider could meet them. Retain an in-house comparator in the decision; there is no scored in-house profile in this model.
