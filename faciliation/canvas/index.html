A facilitation canvas

Choose a workshop you are planning. Work through the twelve prompts with somebody who sees its purpose differently. The agenda comes eleventh. By then you have a reason for what goes into it.

Print a blank sheet, or type in the boxes first. Your notes stay in this tab. Save a copy before you close it.

Planning

Who and what does the work require?

Whose experience, authority, or help will the group need before, during, and after the session?

What would tell you the group is ready? What can reasonably wait until you meet?

Would a particular case, an object, a piece of evidence, or a question help people get to work?

What does the room or online set-up make easy? Who might struggle to hear, speak, or take part?

Vibes

What’s it like to be in this room?

How could the session make things worse? What would you notice early enough to act?

What are you taking for granted about these people or this problem? Who could challenge that?

Write your purpose in a sentence without an ‘and’. What purposes might others bring?

What agreements about disagreement, confidentiality, and decisions would let people do the work?

Process

What will people do, produce, and learn?

What should people be able to understand, decide, or do differently when they leave?

What needs to exist by the end of the session? Who will use it, and when?

Which sequence gives people time to think, hear one another, and reach the decision you agreed?

When will the group find out what happened afterwards? Who can revise the plan in light of that?
