Worked example

‘Can you get the team to buy in?’

Suppose a service manager has agreed a new referral route. The launch date is fixed. Staff still have a choice about what happens when a referral arrives with information missing. There’s a workshop worth having on that question.

Before the invitation
Ask who can authorise a trial. Invite someone who sends referrals, someone who receives them, and someone who’s had to tell their story twice. Say in the invitation which decision is theirs.
In the room
Follow one anonymised referral. Where did somebody have to start again? If the two teams give different accounts, put both on the page. The difference may explain why a sensible rule is producing an absurd result.
Back at work
Agree who’ll call whom on the next incomplete referral. After five referrals, ask the people involved whether anyone still had to repeat their story. Book that review now and agree who can revise the arrangement.
